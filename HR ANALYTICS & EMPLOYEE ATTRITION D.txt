OVERALL CONCLUSION

The analysis shows that employee attrition is concentrated in specific employee segments, particularly Job Level 1, Research & Development, and the 25–34 age group.

Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives, and Research Scientists have the highest attrition counts among job roles.

The dashboard provides an interactive way to analyze employee attrition patterns across departments, job roles, job levels, age groups, overtime, job satisfaction, gender, and business travel.

IMPORTANT NOTE

The percentages presented in these insights represent the share of total attrition, not category-specific attrition rates.

Category-specific attrition rates would require the total employee count for each category.
